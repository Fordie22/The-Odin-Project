Meatballs recipe:


A simple, quick set of meatballs good for any spaghetti.


Ingredients:

  • Jelly
  • Chili sauce
  • Cayenne
  • Meatballs

Steps:

  1. Gather the ingredients.
  2. Combine grape jelly, chili sauce, and cayenne pepper in a saucepan over medium-hgh heat; cook until war, 5 to 10 minutes.
  3. Place meatballs in a slow cooker and top with grape jelly mixture.
  4. Cook on Low for 3 to 4 hours.
  5. Serve warm and enjoy.
