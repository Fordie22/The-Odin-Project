Hamburger recipe:



This hamburger is an easy to make food item for a quick and efficient home meal designed for maximum protein intake.

Ingredients:

  • Lettuce
  • Beetroot
  • Hamburger Buns
  • Cheese
  • Meat paddy
  • Tomato sauce

Steps:

  1. Heat a standard pan until it smokes.
  2. Place the paddy in the pan and lower the heat.
  3. Place some cheese on the paddy.
  4. Once the paddy is firm take it out of the pan and place the buns in a toaster and toast them.
  5. Place the paddy in the toasted bun and cut some lettuice and some beetroot.
  6. Pour some tomato sauce on the paddy and close the burger.
  7. Enjoy.
